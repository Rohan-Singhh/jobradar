Find the jobs worth applying to, ranked against your resume

Job Radar watches the career pages of companies like Stripe, Notion, Databricks, Adobe and Atlassian, reads every opening, and scores each one from 0 to 10 against your resume, with a one-line reason. New roles and closed ones show up the moment they change.

Open the app Run it on your own computer

How it works

  1. Add your resume and an API key. A free key from xkiro is enough. Both stay in your browser.
  2. Press Scan. Job Radar pulls every open role from the tracked company boards and has a language model read each one against your resume, 25 at a time.
  3. Work down the list. Hover a tile to see why it scored what it did, then open the posting. Come back later and only the new roles need scoring.

Scores that respect your level

A model asked to be careful about seniority is not always careful, so two rules are enforced in code. A Senior, Staff or Principal title cannot score above 3, however well the skills match, and a role that is weak on both skills and field is capped too. Without the first rule, senior roles filled a third of the top 100 and the first job a new graduate could get sat at position 67.

What stays private

Your API key and resume are kept in your own browser. When you scan, the key travels with the request to the AI provider and is dropped as soon as the reply comes back. Nothing you add is written to disk or logged on the server, and the app has a button that wipes it from your browser too.
